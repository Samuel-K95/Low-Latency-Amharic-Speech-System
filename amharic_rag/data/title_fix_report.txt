AMHARIC LABOUR PROCLAMATION NO. 1156/2019 — TITLE & SPOKEN TEXT FIX REPORT
================================================================================

SUMMARY OF FIXES:
1. Title-Body Merging Bug (TASK 2):
   - Heading lines in clean.py are now detected on raw lines before paragraph joining.
   - A heading line is only joined with the next line if it meets the full-width threshold (>= 90% of the 80th percentile of line length, ~20.7 chars) and satisfies multi-line title criteria.
   - Single-line headings are emitted as isolated paragraphs, preventing article body text from merging into the title.
   - Post-parsing safety check enforces no sentence marks (።, ፡፡) and <= 12 words in titles.
   - For single-body articles without sub-article markers, sub-articles contain only the body text without title duplication.

2. Spoken Cross-Reference Bug (TASK 3):
   - In src/spoken.py, parenthesized numbers followed by parenthesized letters (e.g. (፬)(ሀ)) and letter ranges (e.g. ፳፯(ለ-ተ)) are properly expanded into '<number words> ፊደል <letter>' and '<number words> ከፊደል <l1> እስከ <l2>'.
   - Glued letter patterns such as 'አራትሀ' and 'ሰባትለ' are completely eliminated across all articles and chunks.
